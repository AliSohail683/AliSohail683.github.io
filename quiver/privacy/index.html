Quiver — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Quiver is an arrow puzzle game. It has no accounts and we, the developer, do not receive any personal information about you. The game is free and shows ads, which are provided by Google AdMob. This page explains what that means.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the level you have reached, your coins and stars, your daily-puzzle streak, and your theme and sound settings — is saved on your device only. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game collects and shares with Google and its advertising partners:

  • device identifiers — on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, otherwise an app-specific identifier; on Android the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps), and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. The game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The game works the same whichever you choose; ads may just be less relevant.

Children

Quiver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released.
