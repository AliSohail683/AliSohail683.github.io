Quiver — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Quiver is an arrow puzzle game. This policy is short because the game collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Quiver has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the level you have reached, your coins and stars, your daily-puzzle streak, and your theme and sound settings — is saved on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Quiver is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this — for example ads or online features — this page will be updated before that version is released.
