General guidance, not veterinary or behavioural advice

The checklist and tips are general, positive-reinforcement guidance for introducing puppies to everyday experiences. They are not veterinary advice or a substitute for a qualified trainer or behaviourist. Every puppy is different. Go slowly, never force an experience, and stop if your puppy is frightened. If your puppy is very fearful, aggressive or reactive, talk to your vet or a qualified professional.

Vaccinations and health

Before your puppy is fully vaccinated, it can be at risk in places where other dogs go. Ask your vet when it is safe for your puppy to walk on the ground, meet unfamiliar dogs and visit public places, and follow their advice over anything in the app. You are responsible for your puppy’s health and safety, and for supervising it at all times, especially around children and other animals.
