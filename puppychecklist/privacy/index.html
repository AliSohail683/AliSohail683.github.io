Puppy Socialization Checklist — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The app has two parts. The puppy checklist works with no account and keeps everything on your phone. Playdates is optional: if you sign in, a small amount of information is stored online so other dog owners nearby can find you and message you. This page explains both.

Part 1: The checklist (on your phone only)

If you only use the checklist, nothing leaves your device:

  • your puppy’s name, breed and birth date, the experiences you log, your notes and reactions;
  • photos you add to the journal, copied into the app’s private storage;
  • your reminder settings (reminders are scheduled locally, not sent from a server).

Deleting the app, or using Settings › “Start over with a new puppy”, deletes this. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple handles that backup under its own privacy terms.

Part 2: Playdates (only if you sign in)

You sign in with Apple or Google. We never see your password. The sign-in service tells us a random account ID and, depending on what you share with it, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). The checklist keeps working the same whether or not you sign in.

Location is requested only “while using the app”, once, when you set up or update your profile. We don’t track you in the background.

Where it’s stored, and who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores playdates data on servers in the United States.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases) and Google (Google Sign-In) process your sign-in.

We don’t use advertising or analytics, and we don’t sell or share your information with anyone else. Photos of your dog are shrunk to a small image and stored inside your profile.

Safety and moderation

Anyone can report or block another person. We review reports and may remove content or accounts that break the Community Rules in our Terms. Reports (who reported whom, why, and where) are kept for as long as needed to keep people safe, even after either person deletes their account.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can edit your profile, hide it, or delete your playdates account at any time in the app: Playdates › Me › Delete my playdates account. This permanently deletes your dog’s profile, requests, chats, blocks and sign-in. For Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the connection to this app. Your checklist on your phone is not affected. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Chats are shared with the other person, so deleting your account ends those chats for both of you.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card, name or Apple ID. The app only checks with the App Store that you own the unlock.

Children

The checklist is for dog owners and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Playdates is for adults aged 18 or over, and we don’t knowingly allow anyone younger to use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app’s App Store privacy details will be kept in line with it.
