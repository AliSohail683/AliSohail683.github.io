Puppy Socialization Checklist — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Puppy Socialization Checklist helps you introduce your puppy to everyday experiences and keep a journal of how it went. It has no account or sign-up, and the developer does not collect your personal information. This page explains what the app does with the information you enter.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved on your device only:

  • your puppy’s name, breed and birth date (or age);
  • the experiences you log, your puppy’s reactions and your notes;
  • photos you add to the journal, which are copied into the app’s private storage on your device;
  • your reminder settings.

We do not receive, copy or store any of this on a server. Deleting the app, or using Settings › “Start over with a new puppy”, deletes it. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup (which can include the app’s data) is handled by Apple under its own privacy terms.

Photos and camera

The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a journal entry. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not come from a server, and we do not receive your notification settings. You can turn them off in the app or in your device’s Settings.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card, name or Apple ID. The app checks with the App Store whether you own the unlock so that it can unlock the full checklist and restore it on a new device.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • No accounts, and no sharing or selling of information, because we hold none.

Children

The app is for dog owners and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds features that use an online service), the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app’s App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
