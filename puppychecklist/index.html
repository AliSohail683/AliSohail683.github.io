Puppy Socialization Checklist

Give your puppy a confident start, one gentle experience at a time.

Puppies learn what is safe in the world during their first sixteen weeks or so. Puppy Socialization Checklist gives you 115 everyday experiences to introduce in that window (people, sounds, surfaces, places, animals, objects, handling and life skills), a tip for each one, a weekly plan, and a journal of how your puppy reacted.

Using the app

  • Log an experience. Open an item, tell the app how your puppy reacted (happy, unsure or scared), and save. Items where your puppy was scared are suggested again later, gently.
  • The weekly plan on the Today screen picks twelve items, favouring the categories you have done least.
  • Reminders. The daily reminder suggests one experience to try. You can change the time or turn it off in Settings.
  • Starting over. Settings › “Start over with a new puppy” deletes your puppy and journal from the device. Your unlock is kept.

The full checklist

The first 26 experiences are free. A one-time purchase unlocks all 115, weekly plans from the whole list, and photos in the journal. It is not a subscription, and there is nothing to renew or cancel. To get it back on a new device or after reinstalling, open the unlock screen and tap “Restore purchase”.

Is my puppy ready for outings?

The app can’t tell you. Ask your vet when your puppy’s vaccinations allow walks where unfamiliar dogs go. Until then, carry your puppy or stick to places that are safe for an unvaccinated puppy.
