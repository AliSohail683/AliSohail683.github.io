Gloop Rush

Swallow, mutate, squeeze, gulp.

You are a jelly blob escaping the lab. Drag to steer, swallow anything smaller than you, pick a mutation at every gate, squeeze through walls, and gulp the vault boss at the end of each level.

Help

  • Green enemies are smaller than you: eat them. Red ones are bigger: dodge them. Yellow is too close to call.
  • The floor under each hole in a wall turns green if you fit, amber if you will have to shed some jelly, red if you can't get through.
