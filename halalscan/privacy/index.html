Halal Scan — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Halal Scan checks whether packaged food is halal from its barcode or a photo of its ingredient list. It has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. This page explains what happens to the information the app does use.

Barcode scans

When you scan or type a barcode, the app looks the product up directly in the public Open Food Facts database. Only the barcode number is sent. The halal check itself runs on your phone. We do not receive or store your barcode scans.

Ingredient-label photos

When you photograph an ingredient list, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Anthropic, the company whose AI model reads the text. The photo is used only to transcribe the ingredients. We do not store it, and Anthropic does not use it to train its models. We keep the transcribed ingredient text, so that misread labels can be studied and fixed.

What we store

  • An anonymous app ID. On first use the app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It is how we count your free label checks and remember a subscription.
  • Label transcriptions, as described above.
  • Reports you send. If you tap “Report” or “Yes” on a result, we store your answer with the barcode or label, the verdict and your settings, to improve the ingredient database.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, we store the subscription details Apple signs for us (product, start and expiry date) so that Pro features work. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID.

What stays on your device

Your scan history, label photos and settings (including your school of thought) are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We don’t sell or share your information with anyone, except the service providers named here, who process it to run the app.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase hosts our server functions and database (United States).
  • Anthropic runs the AI model that reads label photos.
  • Open Food Facts provides product data for barcode lookups.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases.

Keeping and deleting data

Label transcriptions and reports are kept while they help improve the service. To have the data linked to your app ID deleted, email us with the approximate dates of your scans and we will remove it.

Children

Halal Scan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
