Halal Scan

Is it halal? Scan the barcode and see why.

Halal Scan checks packaged food. Scan a barcode or photograph the ingredient list, and it tells you whether the product is Halal, Not halal or Doubtful, with the exact ingredient behind the answer and what to look for on the pack. Rulings that scholars differ on follow the school of thought you choose.

Support

Questions, a wrong result, or a problem with a subscription? Email alisohail6835@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you. If a result looks wrong, tap “Report” on the result screen too: it helps us fix the ingredient database.

Tips for a good label photo

  • Fill the frame with the ingredient list only.
  • Hold the phone flat over the text, in good light.
  • On curved packs, photograph the list in two halves.

Subscriptions

Barcode scans are always free. Manage or cancel Halal Scan Pro in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Pro screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”.
