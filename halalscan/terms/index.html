A guide, not a religious ruling

Halal Scan compares a product’s ingredient list with a database of rulings drawn from published halal-certification guidance, adjusted for the school of thought you choose. It is a guide, not a fatwa. It cannot see how a product was made or how meat was slaughtered, product data can be incomplete or out of date, recipes change, and labels can be misread. Check the pack, the maker or a recognised halal certification, and ask a qualified scholar when it matters to you. You are responsible for what you choose to eat.
