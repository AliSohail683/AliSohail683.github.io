Ironmark

Identify and value vintage cast iron

Photograph the bottom of a cast iron pan and Ironmark reads its markings: the maker, the logo era, the size and pattern numbers, the condition, and what similar pieces typically sell for. It covers Griswold, Wagner, Lodge and other American makers.

Tips for a good scan

  • Turn the pan over and fill the frame with the bottom.
  • Use daylight from the side so raised letters cast small shadows.
  • Wipe off dust and loose rust first.
