Ironmark — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Ironmark identifies vintage cast iron cookware from photos and estimates its value. It has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. This page explains what happens to the information the app does use.

Photos you scan

When you scan a pan, the photos you choose are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Anthropic, the company whose AI model reads the markings. The photos are used only to produce your result. We do not store them on our server, and Anthropic does not use them to train its models. Your photos stay in the app on your device, where you can delete them.

What we store

  • An anonymous app ID. The first time you scan, the app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It is how we count your free scans and remember a subscription.
  • Scan results. For each scan we keep the result (for example the maker, logo era, size, condition and value range) and, if you give it, your answer to “Was this right?”. We use this to improve Ironmark’s accuracy.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, we store the subscription details Apple signs for us (product, start and expiry date) so that Pro features work. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID.

What stays on your device

Your collection, notes, photos and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We don’t sell or share your information with anyone, except the service providers named here, who process it to run the app.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions (United States).
  • Anthropic runs the AI model that reads your photos.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases.

Keeping and deleting data

Scan results are kept while they help improve the service. To have the results linked to your app ID deleted, email us from the app’s support page with the approximate dates of your scans and we will remove them.

Children

Ironmark is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
